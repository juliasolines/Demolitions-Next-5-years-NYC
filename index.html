WHAT COULD THE NEXT 5 YEARS OF NEW YORK CITY’S DEMOLITIONS LOOK LIKE?

The following map explores the 5 years of demolitions in NYC.

A machine learning model trained on data from 2010-2023 from the NYC Housing Database, the NYC Pluto Database and the NYC Census Database flagged buildings that could (with 70% accuracy) undergo demolition from 2025-2030. Each gray circle represents a demolished construction. The reasons for demolitions include year of building construction, rent cost, BBL, address, borough and tenant tax brackets.

We encourage you to find buildings you know, buildings you care about and love, and protect them. Our homes are an extension of our bodies. We are our city, and it is being demolished.
